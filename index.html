A LITTLE REASSURANCE

Good to know.

Do I need to go to the gym or play a sport to book?

No. Our main focus is gym-goers and people who play sports, but the same 60-minute full-body massage is open to anyone looking for leisure and relaxation.

Is my appointment confirmed when I submit?

Your form sends a booking request. Our team will call or WhatsApp you in approximately 10 minutes to confirm availability, the final price and your specialist masseuse. Your appointment is booked once we confirm these details with you.

What do I need for an at-home session?

A quiet, comfortable space with room to move around. We’ll confirm what equipment and supplies are included, and what you need to prepare, when arranging your session.

Can I choose the pressure or focus areas?

Yes. Share your preferences when we confirm your appointment, and discuss comfortable pressure with your therapist before the session begins.

Can I change or cancel my request?

Yes. Call 6306754280 or WhatsApp us to change or cancel your request. Any cancellation terms will be shared before you confirm your appointment.

Which areas do you serve?

Add your city and area to the booking form. We’ll confirm whether a therapist is available at your location before accepting your appointment.
