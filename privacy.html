YOUR DETAILS, HANDLED WITH CARE

Privacy notice

The booking form collects your name, phone number, city and area, session length, date and time. Email and notes are optional.

Why we ask

These details let BookMyMasseuse respond to your request by phone or WhatsApp, check service availability and arrange your appointment. If you choose to provide an email address, we may also use it for booking follow-up. The contact consent in this form relates to your booking request.

Where your request goes

When this website is hosted on Netlify with form handling enabled, submissions are processed and stored through Netlify Forms and are available to the site operator. Netlify may also process technical information to operate the service and prevent spam. Read Netlify’s privacy policy for details.

Keep your notes simple

Please do not include payment information, identification documents or sensitive medical details in this form. Discuss any information needed for your appointment directly with the team when they contact you.

Questions or changes

You can ask the team to correct or delete your booking details by calling 6306754280 or messaging us on WhatsApp.
